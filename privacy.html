Privacy Policy

What's for Dinner · Effective November 26, 2026

What's for Dinner does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your data and iCloud

Your recipes, photos, notes, tags, meal plans, shopping lists and app settings are saved on your device and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage so they appear on your other iPhones and iPads. Apple stores that copy under your Apple Account; we can't see it and it is never sent to us or anyone else. You can turn this off in Settings → your name → iCloud. Deleting the app from one device leaves your iCloud copy for your other devices.

Recipe pages

When you save a recipe from a link or from Safari's Share button, the app loads that page directly from the website you chose, the same way a browser would, and downloads the recipe's photo. These requests contain no personal information from the app. Like any web request, they reveal your device's IP address to that website, which handles it under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

The free trial and the unlock are handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information.

Sharing your list

If you share your shopping list, it goes only to the app or person you choose in the iOS share sheet.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
