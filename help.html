Add a recipe from Safari

  1. Open the recipe page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap What's for Dinner. The first time, you may need to scroll to the end of the row of apps, tap More, and turn it on.
  4. Check the preview, add tags like Main or BBQ if you want, and tap Save.
  5. Open What's for Dinner. The recipe is in your library.

Add a recipe from a link

  1. Copy the recipe's link in any app (Chrome, Messages, Mail).
  2. On the Recipes tab, tap + and choose Paste Recipe Link.
  3. Tap Paste, then Get Recipe.
  4. Check the preview and tap Save.

Some apps can also share a link straight to What's for Dinner.

Type in a recipe

  1. Tap + and choose Type In a Recipe.
  2. Enter ingredients one per line and steps one per line.
  3. Start a line with # to make a heading, like “# For the sauce”.
  4. Add a photo, tags and notes, then tap Save.

Cook with the recipe

  1. Open a recipe and turn your iPhone sideways: ingredients on the left, steps on the right, each scrolling on its own. On iPad they're side by side either way.
  2. Tap an ingredient to check it off. Tap Reset to clear the checks.
  3. Tap a step to highlight where you are.
  4. The screen stays awake while a recipe is open. Turn this off, or turn on larger text, in Settings or the ⋯ menu.

Organize your recipes

  1. Tag recipes by Type, Style, Method, Diet and Occasion when you save or edit them.
  2. Tap Catalog on the Recipes tab to filter by tags or favorites.
  3. Search by name, ingredient, tag or website.
  4. Tap the heart to favorite a recipe, and rate it in Edit.
  5. Can't decide? Tap the dice for a random pick.

Plan your week

  1. On a recipe, tap the calendar+ button, pick a day and meal, and tap Add.
  2. Or on the Meal Plan tab, tap Plan a meal under any day. You can also add a note like “Leftovers”.
  3. Use the arrows at the top to move between weeks.
  4. Press and hold a meal to change the meal slot or make more (2× the recipe), or swipe to remove it.

Make a shopping list

  1. On the Meal Plan tab, tap the cart button, pick the days, and tap Create.
  2. Or on a recipe, tap Add to List and untick anything you already have.
  3. Matching ingredients are combined and sorted by aisle. Salt, pepper and water are skipped (change this in Settings).
  4. Type in the box at the top to add your own items, like “2 lb chicken thighs”.
  5. Tap items to check them off. Press and hold to move an item to another aisle.
  6. Tap the share button to send the list to Messages, Reminders or Notes.

Free trial and unlock

  1. Everything is free for 7 days after you start the trial.
  2. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks adding recipes, meal planning and shopping lists for good. It's not a subscription.
  3. Recipes you've saved stay readable either way.
  4. Bought it already, or on a new device? Go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

If a recipe doesn't import

  1. If a site asks you to confirm you're human, finish the check on the page that appears. The recipe loads after that.
  2. Some sites don't publish their recipe in a format apps can read. You can still save the link and type the recipe in.
  3. To try a saved link again, open the recipe and choose Re-import From Link in the ⋯ menu.

Still stuck? Email support@squishybyteworks.com. If a recipe page doesn't import, please include its link.
