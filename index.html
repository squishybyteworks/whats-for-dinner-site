Just the recipe. Then dinner.

What's for Dinner saves recipes from food blogs without the life story, shows ingredients and steps side by side while you cook, plans your week, and turns it all into one shopping list. For iPhone and iPad.

What it does

  • Save recipes from Safari's Share button or a pasted link: ingredients, steps, photo, servings and cook times, with a link back to the original
  • Cook side by side: turn your iPhone sideways for ingredients on the left and steps on the right, each in its own scrolling panel
  • Organize by type, style, method, diet and occasion, with search, favorites and ratings
  • Plan your week with breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks
  • One shopping list, combined across recipes and sorted by aisle

Adding a recipe from a website

From Safari:

  1. Open the recipe page and tap the Share button.
  2. Tap What's for Dinner. The first time, you may need to scroll to the end of the row of apps, tap More, and turn it on.
  3. Check the preview, add tags if you like, and tap Save. The recipe is in your library next time you open the app.

From any other app: copy the recipe's link, then in What's for Dinner tap + → Paste Recipe Link, tap Paste, then Get Recipe.
